# 🚀 Smart Career Engine

### Competency • Career • Learning

Smart Career Engine is a Python-based career guidance and employment platform prototype designed to help candidates understand their skills, identify skill gaps, discover suitable job roles, follow personalized learning paths, track their progress, and evaluate their career readiness.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many students and job seekers face difficulty in understanding:

- Which skills they currently possess
- Which skills are required for their target job
- What skill gaps they need to improve
- Which jobs are suitable for their current competency
- What courses they should learn next
- Whether they are ready to apply for a particular role

Smart Career Engine addresses these challenges through an integrated competency, career recommendation, and learning system.

---

## 💡 Solution

The system follows a complete career-development cycle:

```text
Candidate Profile
       ↓
Competency Diagnostic
       ↓
Skill Score
       ↓
Job Recommendation
       ↓
Skill Gap Analysis
       ↓
Personalized Learning Path
       ↓
Learning Progress Tracking
       ↓
Adaptive Learning
       ↓
Skill Re-assessment
       ↓
Skill Verification
       ↓
Career Readiness
       ↓
Career Dashboard
